Welcome to Our Anti-Bullying Board Game

This interactive board game was created as part of a psychology class project to raise awareness about bullying and promote empathy among players. Through engaging gameplay, players learn about the impact of bullying and develop strategies to combat it.

The game combines education with entertainment, making it a powerful tool for schools, families, and communities to discuss this important topic in an accessible and meaningful way.
